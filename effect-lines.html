How to use ↓
How to use
  1. Pick a type: 27 types at the top right (focus lines / effect lines / cross-hatch / tones / mood). 🎲 New shape gives another variation of the same type (the same seed always gives the same shape).
  2. Move the center: for focus lines, flash rays, black flash, urchin flash, curved lines, wind, black edge (circle) and rope hatching (radial or circular flow), drag inside the preview (or use the “Center” sliders). The center can even go outside the frame. Focus lines always run past the edge of the frame.
  3. Adjust the shape: the sliders under “Adjust” control line count, inner radius, length, width, variation, clustering and more. Tones have spacing and density; the pattern tone offers 19 patterns. Cross-hatching has single to quadruple layers, edge fade, solid black and flow; black edge has the black shape (line / circle) and fade width. Rope hatching overlaps bundles and draws only the top bundle where they overlap (so the density stays even), with stacking, flow (one direction / radial / circle), line shape (curved / straight), waviness, crossing, lines per bundle, center gap (radial) and gradient (thinner lines, shaped like a Levels adjustment with start / midpoint / end). For the hatching types, “Rotate all” turns only the drawing inside while the frame stays put.
  4. Stack: add layers with + under “Layers”, such as focus lines over halftone or a tone inside a black flash. Color, opacity and whether white areas are transparent are set per layer.
  5. Gacha: “🎰 Gacha” shows alternatives for the selected layer (4, 9 or 16 picks; new shapes with slightly varied settings). Click one to load it into that layer. “🧬 Mutate current” gives slight variations of the current shape. Some types are heavy, so the picks are drawn one at a time.
  6. Save: transparent PNG (1× / 2×), SVG, a ZIP by layer, or copy. A share link passes your settings to someone else.
  • In Clip Studio Paint, load the PNG and set the layer blending mode to “Multiply” to drop the white and keep only the lines (a transparent PNG works as is). For SVG, use File → Import → Vector.
  • Use “Underlay…” to place your art or a panel-layout PNG underneath, so you can line up the focus point or match tone density (the image is not included when saving).
  • Tone spacing is in px. For print, 60 lpi ≈ 10px at 600 dpi and ≈ 6px at 350 dpi. The preview is scaled down, so check how the halftone looks in the saved PNG.
  • Your settings are saved in this browser, so you can pick up where you left off next time.

Everything runs in your browser: your images and underlays are never sent anywhere. The layout and effect types follow the Effect Lines & Screentone Generator by MIYADAIKU (Oyasumi Game Dev), rebuilt here for this personal suite.
