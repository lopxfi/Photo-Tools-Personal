Frame photos, split them for a swipe post, scrub hidden data, add captions, speech bubbles and manga lettering, lay out comic pages, take out backgrounds, and turn chats, comics and stories into videos.

Photos

6 tools

Photo Frame Studio

Put a photo or video in a frame: posts, phone screens, covers, prints and more.

Photo Splitter

Cut one photo into 2 to 4 pieces that join back up as you swipe through your post.

Watermark

Put your name or logo on one photo or a whole batch at once.

Metadata Scrubber

Remove prompts, location and hidden data from photos and videos, and save clean copies.

Background Remover

Take the background out of photos and videos, or key out a green screen.

Outfit Tags

Turn the outfit in a photo or anime art into Danbooru tags and prompts. Uses an AI model.

Keep it on your phone

Install Photo Tools on this device. It gets its own icon and works without internet.

Everything runs in your browser. Your photos and videos stay on your device, except in Outfit Tags, which sends the photo you pick to an AI model to read it. To use it offline on Android, open this page in Chrome, tap ⋮ and choose Add to Home screen or Install app. Open each tool once while you’re online so everything gets saved to your phone. Once it’s installed, Photo Tools also shows up when you tap Share on a photo or video in your gallery.
